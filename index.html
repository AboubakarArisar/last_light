LAST LIGHT: a free browser football game

Play LAST LIGHT (Lastlight), an online soccer game where you draw passes, bend shots and score the winning goal. Play directly in your browser with no download.

How to play

Draw from the ball toward a teammate to pass. Complete the required passes, then draw toward the goal to shoot. Curve your line for a curling shot or choose Lifted for a chip.

Football challenges, one goal at a time

Work through 64 career levels, try the Daily Shot, or challenge a friend to beat your goal quality. Practice free kicks, crosses, headers and volleys, then watch your instant replay.

Use a mouse, touch screen or keyboard. You can play as a guest; an optional account saves your career progress across devices.
